# Platforms and formats

Full research with sources: `docs/studio/MARKET.md` (Southern Africa, Nov 2026). Re-check platform limits each year; they change.

| Where | Send | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Status | 720p copy | up to 90 s (2025) | The main channel for small sellers; first frame must read as a still |
| Facebook Reels | 1080x1920 | in-app above 90 s since mid-2025 | Biggest platform everywhere in the region; Eswatini is almost only Facebook. Link in the first comment |
| TikTok | 1080x1920 | 15 to 60 s for reach | Largest adult reach in South Africa; growing fastest in Mozambique |
| YouTube Shorts | 1080x1920 | up to 3 min (vertical/square) | Our music is our own, so no Content ID blocks |
| YouTube (long) | `compile.cjs` landscape | 8 to 10 min | Watch hours toward the Partner Program (1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours, or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days; stricter rules announced from Feb 2027) |
| Instagram Reels | 1080x1920 | 15 to 90 s | Only worth it in Namibia and South Africa |
| Posters | `--stills` frame | still | WhatsApp, Facebook, print; QR codes must be real ones (the cartoon QR is a drawing) |
| Thumbnails | from `compile.cjs` | 1280x720 | Big readable words, one face |

## Audience by country (social media identities, Oct 2025)
Eswatini 513 k (40.7%), South Africa 29.1 m (44.9%), Botswana 1.40 m (54.4%), Namibia 797 k, Lesotho 653 k, Zimbabwe 2.60 m, Zambia 4.10 m, Malawi 2.40 m, Mozambique 4.10 m (Portuguese). Median age 18 to 29: write for young adults.

## Posting rhythm
Daily short, two episodes a week, a compilation every two weeks; best times 06:30 to 07:30, 15:00 to 16:30, 20:30 to 21:45, Sunday 17:00 to 19:00 (Servo: avoid restaurant rush hours). Every post gets a link tag (`vk-n04`, `sv-s01`, ...); results go into `feedback.md`.
