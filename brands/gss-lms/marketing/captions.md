# Captions and links

Generated by `node marketing/captions.mjs`. Put the **link in the first comment** on Facebook (not in the post). Every link carries a tag, so the console (Growth → Campaign) shows how many people opened the demo from each post. Pictures: `marketing/out/<set>/<id>-1x1.png` (feed) and `-9x16.png` (WhatsApp Status, Reels). Videos: `marketing/out/video/<set>-<size>.mp4`.

Rules for every post: say **demo data**; no invented numbers or testimonials; nothing the app does not do; one idea, one call to action (open the demo).
