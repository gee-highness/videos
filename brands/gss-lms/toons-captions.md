# GSS-LMS cartoon shorts: post captions

One caption per short (Facebook Reels, WhatsApp Status, TikTok, YouTube Shorts). Put the link in the first comment on Facebook. Tags are counted in console → Growth → Campaign. Plan and posting order: PLAN.md.

## GS01 Blessed Tuesday (for principals)

How many learners are absent today? At Cartoon High, the answer arrives as a Blessed Tuesday flower picture. 🌸

With GSS-LMS, teachers take the register on their phones and the principal sees who is absent, and which register is not done yet.
