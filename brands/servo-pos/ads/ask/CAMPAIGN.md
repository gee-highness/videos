# Servo POS: "What would your favourite restaurant's app do?" (ask the public)

A two-week campaign aimed at **diners, not owners**. It sits on top of `docs/POSTING_PLAN.md` (rules, tags, tone) and runs before or alongside `ads/launch/`.
The post itself (copy, first comment, reply scripts) is in `POST.md`; images are in `images/`.

## 1. Why ask the public
1. **Learn:** which features diners actually want, in their own words, before we build more.
2. **Pull:** a restaurant owner who hears "your guests are asking for this" says yes far more often than one who is cold-pitched. Diners nominate the restaurants; we bring the owner a list of what *their* guests asked for.
3. **Audience:** people who comment and vote are the first audience for the guest app of every founding restaurant.

**One goal:** collect real wishes and restaurant names. **One ask per post:** answer one question.

| What | 2-week target | How you see it |
|---|---|---|
| People reached | 3,000 to 10,000 | Facebook Insights |
| Comments / poll votes | 150 to 300 | the post and the poll |
| **Restaurants nominated** (distinct names) | **30 to 60** | the nominations sheet (section 6) |
| Survey completions (optional form) | 60 to 120 | the form's responses |
| Owners contacted from nominations | 10 | your notes |

Targets, not predictions. If reach is fine but comments are thin by day 5, change the **question**, not the volume.

## 2. Who we ask
- Diners 18 to 45 in **Mbabane, Manzini, Matsapha, Ezulwini** (then Nhlangano, Siteki, Piggs Peak): students, young professionals, families who eat out or order takeaway at least twice a month.
- Reach them where they already are: **Facebook** (page + local food, student and community groups), **WhatsApp** (Status and groups you belong to), and **in person** (table tents at friendly restaurants).
- Best times (Eswatini): weekdays **12:30 to 13:30**, **19:30 to 21:30**, Sunday **14:00 to 17:00**. Owners are the opposite: do not expect them in these windows.

## 5. The survey (Google Form, 5 questions, under 90 seconds)
1. **What is your favourite restaurant or takeaway in Eswatini?** (short text, required)
2. **If it had its own app, what would you use first?** (pick up to 2: order ahead / track my order / rewards / book a table or waitlist / full menu with pictures / pay from my phone / something else)
3. **How often do you eat out or order takeaway?** (a few times a week / weekly / a few times a month / rarely)
4. **What annoys you most when you eat out?** (long queue / wrong order / not knowing wait time / paying / booking / other)
5. **Can we tell the restaurant their guests are asking for this?** (yes, no name / yes, with my first name / no)
Optional last line: "Your WhatsApp number, only if you want updates" (never required).

Form intro: "Servo POS is a restaurant system built in Eswatini. We use your answers to decide what to build and to tell restaurants what their guests want. We never publish your name without asking." (Have the data wording reviewed; see section 8.)

## 6. Features to ask about (plain words, and what is true today)
Stay inside `docs/POSTING_PLAN.md` section 3. In public copy, ask *which would you use*; never say a feature exists unless it does.
| Ask diners about | Real status |
|---|---|
| Order ahead from your phone, pick up at the counter | Live (restaurant's customer menu link) |
| Track your order | Live (order tracking page) |
| Rewards and points | Live (restaurant sets its own points and rewards; Standard and up) |
| The restaurant's own app on your phone | Live on Standard and Multi-branch |
| Menu with pictures, allergens | Live |
| Book a table, join a waitlist | Add-on, **coming soon**: say "coming soon", never a date |
| Pay from your phone | Card payments work once a restaurant's card processing is connected; do not promise it in public |

**Nominations sheet** (one row per comment/response): date, source (post/poll/form), restaurant named, town, feature wished for (one of the list or "other"), permission (none / no name / first name), reply sent. Keep it in a private sheet.

## 7. Turning answers into results
1. **Scoreboard** after day 4 and day 14: top 5 wishes, top 10 restaurants. Post the wishes publicly; keep restaurant counts for owner conversations.
2. **Owner pitch** (WhatsApp/email, `docs/POSTING_PLAN.md` section 10 for the follow-up steps): "12 of your guests told us they would use [order ahead] at [Restaurant]. Want to see what it looks like? [demo link]. First 5 restaurants get 6 months free." Share counts and wishes only; first names only with permission.
3. **Roadmap input:** every wish that appears 5+ times and is not built goes on the product list (bookings and waitlist are already planned).
4. **Spotlight:** a restaurant that says yes becomes the `ads/spotlight/` story ("Your favourite restaurant said yes").

## 8. Data and consent
- Collect the minimum: restaurant name, wish, optional first name and WhatsApp number. No ID numbers, no payment details, no children's data.
- Say plainly what it is used for (the form intro above). Never publish a name or comment text without asking; screenshots of comments get names cropped.
- Delete a person's answers on request. Keep the sheet private and in one place.
- Eswatini has a data protection law (2022). **Have the form wording and retention reviewed before collecting phone numbers.** The pages in `src/app/privacy` are drafts.
- A prize draw (for example a restaurant voucher) is optional. If you add one: no purchase needed, published rules, drawn live, and the restaurant must agree in writing. Check local rules first; the campaign works without one.

## 9. Moderation and tone
- Warm, plain English. Add Siswati only if a native speaker checks it first.
- Reply to everything, including criticism. Never argue; ask what would change their mind.
- Do not name or tag a restaurant in a post without their OK. Comments naming restaurants stay in comments.
- No claims we cannot show. "Demo" on every screen. No "fastest", "cheapest", "#1", "works offline".

## 10. Weekly review (20 minutes, Friday)
Look at three numbers: **comments, distinct restaurants named, survey completions**. Then decide:
- Many comments, few restaurant names: change the question to "Which restaurant?" first.
- Many votes, few comments: the poll works, add a "why?" follow-up.
- High reach, low engagement: change the image, not the audience.
- Many nominations for one restaurant: contact that owner this week.
