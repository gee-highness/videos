## 1. The one goal and the one rule
**Goal:** fill the 10 founding spots with restaurants that take real orders.
**Rule:** every post has ONE idea, ONE image, ONE ask. Facebook shows the post, WhatsApp closes the deal.

## 2. How a post turns into a customer (the funnel)
1. **Hook** (image + first line): a pain an owner feels, not a feature list.
2. **See it:** `servo-pos.vercel.app/demo?ref=<tag>` (no sign-up).
3. **Ask:** two doors, so nobody is stuck:
   - *Claim a spot:* `servo-pos.vercel.app/join?ref=<tag>` (name + email).
   - *Talk to a human:* `https://wa.me/26879342380` ("Send us a WhatsApp: 'SPOT'"). Most owners here prefer a chat to a form.
4. **Reply within an hour** (06:30 to 07:30, 15:00 to 16:30, 20:30 to 21:45 are the owners' quiet windows).
5. Then follow section 10 of `POSTING_PLAN.md` (create tenant, welcome pack, day-3 and day-7 check-ins).

**Facebook mechanics:** link in the **first comment** (tagged), not the caption, so reach isn't throttled. Pin the Monday launch post for the whole period. Reply to every comment; a question back ("Which dish sells most for you?") doubles the thread. Reshare each post to WhatsApp Status the same day (`wa-status`). Cover photo = share card; Contact button -> `/join`.

## 5. Daily engine (10 to 20 minutes, around the posts)
- **10 WhatsApp DMs, Mon to Fri** to owners from your list (scripts and replies in `POSTING_PLAN.md` section 8, and quick replies in `WHATSAPP_BUSINESS.md`). Never bulk-add to groups; stop at "no".
- **Comment first on local business and food pages** with something useful, not a link.
- **2 Facebook group posts a week** (only groups that allow promotion; read the rules).
- **Ask 3 partners** (supplier, accountant, chamber) to share the launch post.
- **Boost (optional, week 2):** best-performing post only, Mbabane / Manzini / Matsapha / Ezulwini, ages 25 to 55, small test budget, tag `fb-ad01`.

## 6. What to measure
Join emails say `Source: fb-cNN`; WhatsApp chats should say which post they came from (ask). Track: reach, link clicks, join emails, WhatsApp chats, spots filled, first orders in 7 days. After day 7 do more of the two best tags and drop the weakest. If join emails are far under target, fix the message before adding volume.

## 7. Truth rules (so the brand earns trust)
- Always say "demo" for demo screens. The images use **sample screens and example numbers**, not a real restaurant.
- The own app is **Standard and up only**. Bookings are a paid add-on (E250 / E300) and only mentioned to owners who ask, so there is no bookings post.
- Don't say fastest, cheapest or #1, and never "works offline".
- Only post a spots count from the Founding Program page.
- No restaurant's name or photo without their written OK (a WhatsApp message counts).
