# Spotlight campaign: "Real restaurants on Servo POS"

**Why this one next:** the launch campaign shows what Servo does (demo data). Owners still ask "who else uses it?". Proof is the biggest remaining blocker, so the next campaign is social proof, not more features.

## Start trigger (do not post before)
- At least 2 restaurants have taken real orders on Servo.
- At least 1 has given **written permission** (a WhatsApp message counts) for their name, logo, photos and a quote.
- Review the launch campaign first: keep the 2 best-performing tags' pillars.

## Goal and numbers
Convert the warm audience from the launch: spots 6 to 10 filled, plus 10 new WhatsApp chats. Success = join emails tagged `fb-s..`, first orders in 7 days, 3 more permissions to feature.

## Audience
Owners who saw the launch but didn't join, plus lookalikes in Mbabane, Manzini, Matsapha, Ezulwini (ages 25 to 55).

## Message
"Restaurants like yours already run on Servo." One restaurant, one result, one quote, one call to action. Honest numbers only, from the admin console (orders taken, months live), never invented.

## Rules
Written permission for every name, photo and quote. Say "demo" for demo data. Never promise features not shipped. Link only to `/join?ref=fb-sNN` or WhatsApp.
