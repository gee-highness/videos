## 1. What every video must do
1. **Make the owner laugh at their own day** in the first 3 seconds (a problem they know, not a feature).
2. **Show the turn:** your business gets **its own app**, with your name, logo and prices, that customers **install on their phone**.
3. **Show the tools:** orders, bookings, stock, sales, staff, **from any device** (phone, tablet, laptop).
4. **One clear next step:** the link, on screen for the last 5 seconds.

One idea per video. English. Captions always on (most people watch muted). Every episode is also voiced and has sound effects and music (section 8).

## 2. The style, and why each choice
| Choice | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Look | Black ink on cream paper, line "boils" 5 times a second | Reads as hand-made and friendly; stands out among glossy ads |
| Colour | Black and white world; **only the app is in brand colour** (green for Venda Khona, purple for Servo) | The eye goes to the product without a single arrow |
| Movement | Drawn "on twos" (12 pictures a second), blinks, mouth flaps, pops | Same feel as the reference; fast to render (2 minutes a video) |
| Shape | 1080x1920 vertical, art in the middle, caption box low, link strip at the bottom | WhatsApp Status, Reels, TikTok; nothing important under the app buttons |
| Captions | White brush lettering on a black box, max 7 words a line, key word in brand colour | Legible on a small phone in sunlight |
| Length | 45 to 60 s episodes, 15 s cut-downs | Long enough for a joke and a turn; cut-downs for Status |
| Characters | Our own cast (below), waist up, behind a table or counter | Simple to draw consistently; no copied characters |
| Recurring bit | **"Small Business News"** desk: a deadpan anchor reports the owner's small disaster as breaking news, then the good news | A format people recognise by episode 3; the picture box shows the app |
| Sound | A narrator and the anchor (two different voices), an effect on every visible event, a quiet tune that stops for the news desk | The user asked for voices and for effects "to make everything better"; the dry news desk makes the anchor funnier |
| Ending | Same end card every time: logo, promise, link | Brand memory |
| Honesty | Cartoon shops, said on the end card; no invented numbers or testimonials; offer text exactly as approved | Same rules as all other posts |

## 7. Writing rules (jokes and words)
**The standard (user, 17 Nov 2026): the humour in every video must be the best it can be, and everything must feel natural and human.** Treat this as the test every line and picture has to pass before it goes to the user.

**Humour**
- The joke is always on **the situation**, never on customers, staff or a real business.
- One real, specific detail beats a general one: "turn left at the goat", "2 chkn?? 7 chps", "the price is ON the picture".
- Put the funny word last in the line, then stop. Do not explain the joke.
- Escalate in threes, and let the third one break the pattern.
- Understate. The narrator and Nomsa stay calm while things fall apart; the calmer the voice, the funnier the picture.
- Give characters a short line of their own when it is funnier in their mouth than the narrator's.
- Every episode ends on a callback to its opening problem.
- Write three versions of the hook and of the last joke, keep the best, and say which were dropped if the user asks.

**Natural and human**
- Write how people talk: contractions, short sentences, a pause where a person would pause ("Honestly?", "Okay.", "Sir.").
- No advert language in the voice: not "seamlessly", "all in one place", "boost", "manage your business". Say what happens: "she scans it, and his shop lives on her phone".
- The product part must sound like the same person who told the joke, not a different announcer.
- Read every line aloud in your head at speaking speed. If it sounds written, rewrite it.
- Faces react: a blink, a look, a raised eyebrow before the line lands. Nobody stands frozen while they are spoken about.

**Truth and wording**
- Use things local owners know: E prices, kota, cakes, braids, WhatsApp groups, "hw mch". No Siswati lines until a native speaker checks them.
- Captions: present tense, max 7 words a line, max 2 lines, one highlighted word.
- Say what is true: Venda Khona is free (no fees, no commission). Servo needs an internet connection; plans and free months only as in the posting plan.
- No "fastest", "cheapest", "#1". No numbers presented as results.

1. Each episode file lists its lines in `EP.voice` (number, start time, who, text) and its voices in `EP.voices`.
2. `node toons/voice.cjs <id>` prints the Termux commands (15% faster than the default speed; the first take was too slow). The cloud session cannot reach the Edge voices, so the user pastes the commands into Termux (`pip install edge-tts` once) and sends back `NN.mp3`.
3. Put the files in `toons/voice/<id>/`. `engine.cjs` mixes them in at their times and prints where each line ends; move a line's `at`, or a scene's `from`/`to`, until nothing overlaps. `--audio-only` remixes without redrawing.
4. All voice files are generated 15% faster (`--rate=+15%`); `voiceTempo` in an episode is only for files made at another speed.

The licence of the Edge voices for adverts is unclear (the user chose them knowing this). Azure Speech has the same voices with a clear licence if that is ever needed.

### Sound effects and music
Every episode has effects and a quiet tune, made in code by `toons/sfx.cjs` (nothing downloaded, no licence): `pop` (bubbles, things appearing), `tick` (taps, typing), `buzz` (phone), `ding`, `chime` (success), `whoosh`, `scribble`, `womp` (sad), `sting` (news opener), `munch`.
- `EP.sfx: [[time, name, volume], ...]`: put a sound on every visible event (a bubble, a tap, a pop-in), the `sting` at the news cut and a `ding` at the good news.
- `EP.music: [[from, to], ...]`: the tune plays in these ranges; leave the news desk out so the anchor is dry. `--no-music` renders without it.
- Claude cannot hear the result: after a change, ask the user how the levels sound. Effect level is the `0.32` in `sfx.cjs`; music level is in `pluck(...)`.

### Trying other voices
`voice.cjs <id> N=<voice> A=<voice>` swaps voices for one episode. ## 9g. Automatic timing (episodes from VK04 on)
Episodes set `auto: true`. Nothing is timed by hand: the engine measures every voice file and lays out the whole episode from it, so a re-recorded line just means re-rendering.
- `EP.voice`: `[{ who, text, gap? }]`; the file for line *i* is `voice/<id>/NN.mp3` (NN = i + 1, two digits). A line starts after the previous one ends plus `gap` (default 0.34 s).
- `EP.scenes`: `{ s: firstLineIndex, dry?, tail?, draw(t, T, len, rel) }`. A scene runs from its first line to the next scene's first line. `rel(i)` is when line *i* starts inside the scene, `rel.end(i)` when it ends, `rel.on(i, t)` is true while it is spoken: use them to sync bubbles, mouths and taps to the speech.
- `EP.caps`: `[[lineIndex, text]]`, shown while that line is spoken. `EP.sx`: `[[lineIndex, offsetSeconds, 'sound', volume?]]`. The tune plays in every scene that is not `dry` (the news desk is dry).
- The shared kit `K` (end of `lib.js`): cast (`thandi`, `zanele`, `gama`, `sipho`, `lady`, `gogo`, `man`, `musa`, `lindiwe`, `boss`), props (`win`, `table`, `counter`, `smallPhone`, `buzz`, `mark`, `stamp`, `goat`, `light` (a traffic light, "robot")), and whole scenes: `K.news` (news desk), `K.ownApp` (the shop's own app, scan and install), `K.devices` (phone, tablet and laptop on the counter, with `s1/s2/s3` overrides), `K.end` (end card, `kind: 'vk'|'sv'`).
- A missing or empty voice file is timed from its text and left silent; the engine prints `MISSING voice lines`. Do not send such a video.

## 10. How to make an episode, step by step
**Files**
- `toons/lib.js`: the drawing kit (people, faces, arms, bubbles, phones, QR drawing, captions, paper, the boiling line).
- `toons/ep/<id>.js`: one episode: brand colour, link, logo, voice lines, captions, scenes.
- `toons/engine.cjs <id>`: renders the video. `toons/voice.cjs <id>`: prints the Termux voice commands.
- `toons/voice/<id>/NN.mp3`: the voice files (kept in the repo so a video can be rebuilt).
- `toons/out/`: rendered videos (not in the repo).

**Steps**
1. **Pick the episode** from section 5 or 6 and write its script as a table like section 9: time, picture, caption, narrator line. Follow the beat sheet (section 4) and the writing rules (section 7).
2. **Copy `ep/vk04.js`** (Venda Khona) or `ep/sv03.js` (Servo: purple, its logo and demo link) to `ep/<id>.js` (they use automatic timing, section 9g). Set `id`, `title`, `brand`, `accentOnInk`, `url`, `logoFile` and `voices` (section 8). Use the kit's `newsDesk(...)` for the news scene and `screen(...)` for tablets, monitors and laptops, as `sv01.js` does.
3. **Write `EP.voice`** (number, start time, who, text), `EP.captions` (max 7 words a line, 2 lines, one `*highlighted*` word), `EP.sfx` (a sound on every visible event) and `EP.music` (everything except the news desk).
4. **Draw the scenes.** Each scene is `{ from, to, draw(t) }` and returns `{ art, ui }`: `art` gets the strong wobble (people, furniture), `ui` the light one (lettering, phone screens). Keep art between y 330 and 1400; the caption box sits at about y 1450 to 1670 and the link strip below it. Only the app is drawn in the brand colour.
5. **Check stills** before a full render: `node toons/engine.cjs <id> --stills 1,5,9` and look at each one. Bubbles and captions must not cover faces; nothing may run off the right edge.
6. **Get the voices:** `node toons/voice.cjs <id>`, give the user the printed block to paste into Termux, and put the MP3s they send in `toons/voice/<id>/`.
7. **Render:** `node toons/engine.cjs <id>` (about 2 minutes). It prints when each voice line starts and ends. If two lines overlap or a line runs past its scene, move the line's `at` or stretch the scene's `from`/`to` (and shift the later times by the same amount). Sound only changed: `--audio-only`.
8. **Review:** look at the contact sheet (`toons/out/<id>-sheet.jpg`), confirm the files have sound (`ffmpeg ... -af volumedetect`), and fix what is wrong before sending. Say plainly that the sound was not listened to.
9. **Deliver:** send `<id>.mp4` (1080x1920) and `<id>-720.mp4` (WhatsApp) with SendUserFile, with the post caption (hook, what it shows, the offer, the tagged link, a share nudge, "Cartoon shop").
10. **Record it:** add the script table and caption to this file, tick the episode in section 5 or 6, commit and push.

**Things already learnt**
- A wobble filter over text that runs far off-screen (the news ticker) is drawn at low quality: the filters are fixed to the 1080x1920 frame; keep it that way.
- Changing voice speed inside the same ffmpeg graph as the delay and mix gives silence; the engine speeds each line up in its own step.
- The cloud session cannot reach the Edge voices (the network does not allow that kind of connection); they are made on the user's phone in Termux.
- One very long text that starts far off-screen (the news ticker) is drawn garbled: `tickerText()` in the kit draws each phrase on its own and only when it is on screen. Use it for any scrolling text.
- A new episode for a brand can reuse whole scenes (own app, devices, end card) from the brand's first episode: `sv02.js` was built that way.
- Give the user the voice lines first and draw while they generate them: the lines set every scene's length.
- For Servo, say "Standard plan and up" whenever the restaurant's own app is shown or spoken, and put "Servo needs an internet connection" on the end card.
- Masters are encoded at CRF 24: the paper grain makes lower values very large.
