# Plan: [WORKING TITLE]

Fill this in before writing a word of script. A video that is planned well is easy to pace.

## 1. Who and why
- **Viewer:** who is this for, in one line? (Default: a curious adult with no special knowledge. If a 15-year-old and a manager would both follow it, it is pitched right.)
- **What they already believe:** the common idea the video will challenge.
- **What they leave with:** one sentence they could repeat to a friend.
- **Why now:** the news, report or moment that makes it timely.

## 2. Title and thumbnail
- 3 title options (a big claim plus a feeling or a number; no lies): 1. … 2. … 3. …
- Thumbnail: one image + at most 3 words.

## 3. Sources (every number on screen or in the narration)
| Claim, exactly as we will say it | Source (title, publisher, year, page) | Checked |
|---|---|---|

## 4. Shape and timing (145 words a minute)
| Chapter (# line) | Job | Target length | Beat that keeps people watching |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | the promise, the turn, the shock number, the open loop | 0:00 to 0:30 | a number or a question in the first 15 s |
| Part 2 | the evidence | 1 to 2 min | a [card] with the key figure |
| Part 3 | why it happened (2 or 3 reasons) | 2 to 4 min | a question to the viewer, a [pause] before each reason |
| Part 4 | the counterpoint: where it does work | 1 to 2 min | a surprising example |
| Part 5 | the turn and the callback to the first image | 0.5 to 1 min | the opening picture again |

## 5. Engagement checklist
- [ ] Hook: a number, a question or a card within 15 seconds; the open loop is paid off later.
- [ ] Something new on screen at least every 60 seconds (chapter, card, question, pause); the `check` command flags gaps.
- [ ] Shots 4 to 8 seconds; none over 11 (`check` flags them).
- [ ] Every idea has an everyday example anyone can picture (a shop, a school, a phone, a taxi queue).
- [ ] Plain words: no jargon without a one-line explanation; sentences under ~20 words.
- [ ] One "you" moment per chapter: ask the viewer something about their own life.
- [ ] The ending calls back to the opening image and answers the open loop.
- [ ] Every number matches the source table above.
