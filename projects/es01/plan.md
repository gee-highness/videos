## 5. Rules for this video
- No clips from the show. Every quote is on screen as text, so it can be posted anywhere.
- The narrator never says the crude words; they are on screen with an asterisk.
- No real faces in the images: places, rooms and objects tell the story.
- Karen's claims about Amanda's private life are said on air to be an insult, not a fact.
- Every disputed claim from the reunion is followed by the other woman's denial.
