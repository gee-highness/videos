# Plan: The AI Takeover Quietly Stalled

## 1. Who and why
- **Viewer:** anyone who has heard "AI will replace everyone" and half believes it: workers, students, small business owners, managers. No tech knowledge needed.
- **What they already believe:** companies are swapping people for AI and it is working.
- **What they leave with:** "Most company AI projects made no measurable money, because the tools couldn't learn how the work is really done. The ones that worked helped people instead of replacing them."
- **Why now:** the MIT NANDA report (2025) is still the most-quoted number in every AI argument, and it is usually quoted wrong.

## 2. Title and thumbnail
1. The AI Takeover Quietly Stalled. Here's What the Numbers Say
2. 95% of Company AI Made No Money. Here's Why
3. AI Was Supposed to Replace Us. Then It Forgot Everything
- Thumbnail: the lone robot at the CRT desk + "95% NOTHING".
