## Karen Loderick-Peace
- CLAIM (her words): "I'm from Jamaica, a little girl from the countryside. And I came to England at 25 years old." [BR][TK]
- FACT: Founder and CEO of the womenswear label Becuz (BeCuz), launched 2025. [BR][TB2]
- FACT: Married to Jeremy Peace, a businessman and former chairman of West Bromwich Albion (left 2016). Three children. His fortune is reported at about £175m (outlet estimates, not official). [BR][TBG][YH]
- FACT: She has been on every UK Real Housewives show: Jersey, Cheshire and London. [W][TAB-all]
- Age 50 (PEOPLE) or 51 (HELLO!). Say "fifty".
- CLAIM: owns five properties: "I own Chelsea, I own Belgravia and I own two in Jersey." Bravo (citing The Standard) adds a house in Staffordshire. [BR]

## Amanda Cronin
- FACT (reported): 58. Grew up in Hampshire near Winchester. Former model; an agent advised "Amanda" instead of "Mandy". Married First Utility co-founder Mark Daeche in 2013; divorced 2019. [TBG][YP]
- FACT: Runs her own brand, Amanda Caroline (skincare and a Belgravia clinic, The Secret Door). [YH][TB]
- FACT (HELLO!): Her Belgravia mews house: found in 2019, two years of redevelopment, moved in 2022. Built around 1700 as a coaching house; before that it was a two-floor flat above three garages. Three storeys, four bedrooms, five bathrooms. Estimated £12 million. She calls it "the Amanda Caroline Maison, aka Barbie's dream house." [YH]
- In that same HELLO! shoot (Nov 2025) she said: "I wouldn't be surprised if there's another viral one-liner that comes out of series two." [YH]  ← the ending

## What a mews house is
- FACT: Mews houses were built as stables and carriage houses, with rooms for coachmen, behind grand London homes; most were converted into homes in later centuries, and they are now some of London's most sought-after addresses. [YP][BOSSIP citing Britannica][BR]

## Corrections to the first draft script (what changed and why)
- Season 2 has 10 episodes plus a two-part reunion (12 in all), not 10 with a finale at 10. Episode 9 and 10 titles are right.
- The Alba lunch did **not** end in a Karen–Amanda truce: it settled most feuds except theirs.
- "£500,000 into Amanda's brand" was backwards: it was Amanda's jab that Jeremy's money was behind Karen's brand ("500k, and you didn't even get a dress off the ground").
- No source for "ride-or-die", "lackey", Bethenny's "enjoy the ride", or the cast splitting cleanly into two camps: removed.
- The "three hours late" claim and the "go f*** her up" claim were made by Karen at the reunion, and denied by Amanda.
- Amanda's mews house is in Belgravia, not "stuck in Belgravia" (that was about Jersey).
