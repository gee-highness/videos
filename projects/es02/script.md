A man was mauled by three dogs on a Durban street. Now he and the dogs' owner tell police two different stories.

# What happened
[img] a lone recycling trolley parked beside a bin on a quiet leafy road, early light {move:panR}
Wednesday morning, Westville. Sizwe Doncabe was collecting recyclables.

[img] an empty stretch of road, a dropped recycling sack on the tarmac, long shadows {move:out}
[sfx hush]
Three pit bulls attacked him. He is in hospital, and his family says he could lose his right arm.

# The fair part
[host eyebrow]
[music calm]
Now, to be fair, nothing is proven. Police are investigating both accounts.

[img] a police station front desk at night, a lamp, a stack of folders {move:in}
The same three dogs were linked to two earlier attacks, on other dogs. A case of negligently allowing an animal to cause injury is open.

[img] a closed courtroom door, a brass scale on a bench, warm lamplight {move:in}
And the gate matters. In South African law an owner can be liable even without negligence, unless the victim had no right to be there.
