# The hook
[img] illustration: a calabash cup of palm wine on a stool in a spotlight, rows of chairs in the dark, string lights {move:in}
[host full name]
A bride stopped her own traditional wedding. Not over money. Not over family. Over one song.

# The ritual
[img] illustration, top-down: a hall of chairs with a winding path from a cup of palm wine to a hidden heart {move:in}
[sfx chime]
In an Igbo wedding, called igba nkwu, everyone waits for one moment.

[img same] {move:panR}
The bride takes a cup of palm wine and searches the crowd for her husband.

[img same] {move:out}
It usually takes a few minutes. Guests try to distract her. The groom often hides. Then the couple dance.

# The standoff
[img] illustration: a DJ booth, a laptop with a failed download bar, speakers, string lights {move:in}
[music sneaky]
A bride identified as Sugah asked the DJ for one particular song. He said he did not have it.

[img same] {move:out}
He tried to download it. The download failed.

[img] illustration: an empty aisle between chairs, a cup waiting on a decorated chair {move:panR}
[sfx scratch]
So he played another song by the same artist. She said no.

[img] illustration: a house at dusk with a warm lit door, palms, a path {move:in}
She reportedly said the song had been on her mind since her single days, and that she would go back to her father's house unless it was found.

[img same] {move:out}
[shot] press-ghpage.png | GhPage, 24 Nov 2026
Her friends, family, in-laws, even the groom, reportedly begged her to take another one.

# The fair part
[img] illustration: two speech bubbles facing each other, one with a music note, one with a laptop {move:in}
[host eyebrow]
[music calm]
Now, to be fair. Online, people are split. Some say it is her day, and the DJ should be ready. Others say it is one song.

[img] illustration: a record on a turntable with a soft equaliser behind it, calm backdrop for a date card {move:in}
[card] 2024 | Another Igbo bride held back her wine until the DJ played "Obodo"
It is not the first time. In 2024, a video showed another Igbo bride holding back her wine until the DJ played Obodo. He played it. She served it.

# The lesson
[img] illustration: a phone showing a playlist with one song starred, beside a notepad with ticks {move:in}
[music triumph]
Whoever is right, wedding planners say the same thing: tell your DJ your songs, and ask if he can play them, before the day.
